---
title: "Open-RMF"
description: "Expose eligible UMP participants without replacing RMF traffic and facility scheduling."
icon: "route"
---

Open-RMF remains responsible for traffic, maps, doors, lifts, and facility
scheduling. UMP exposes eligible participants through a fleet-adapter bridge and
translates only configured task categories into proposed goals or assignments.

## Boundary

- RMF owns navigation graph participation and facility resources.
- UMP owns manufacturer-neutral semantic awareness and owner authority checks.
- The robot adapter owns final capability acceptance and execution.

```bash
python3 examples/standards_mapping_demo.py open_rmf
ump-integration runtime open_rmf
```

Open-RMF requires its supported ROS 2 installation with `rclpy` and
`rmf_adapter`. The built-in fixture validates mapping and task translation; a
selected RMF deployment must be qualified separately.
