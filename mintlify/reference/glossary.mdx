title: "Glossary"
description: "Precise meanings for UMP protocol and deployment terms."
icon: "book-a"
---

**Adapter** — The manufacturer-controlled semantic boundary implementing
`RobotAdapter`.

**Assignment** — One validated, authority-bound high-level capability request
for one robot.

**Authority lease** — A local, scoped, expiring robot-owner permission for one
issuer and capability.

**Capability** — A versioned high-level semantic contract with validated inputs,
outputs, units, and frames.

**Coordinator** — The durable service that validates plans, checks authority,
dispatches ready assignments, and records outcomes.

**Freshness** — Whether an observed state remains current under configured time
bounds.

**Manifest** — A robot's identity and currently advertised capability contracts.

**Mapping report** — Loss-aware evidence listing mapped, defaulted,
unsupported, and rejected fields during standards conversion.

**Native acceptance** — The robot controller or adapter's final decision to
accept or reject a high-level assignment.

**Participant** — One authenticated robot or coordinator identity on a UMP
network.

**Plan** — A validated set of capability steps and dependencies proposed for a
shared goal.

**State** — A bounded semantic observation of current robot operation.

**Unknown outcome** — Work that may have executed but whose terminal result
cannot be safely proven without reconciliation.
