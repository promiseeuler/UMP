---
title: "Supervised hardware trials"
description: "Move from software evidence to one controlled physical capability at a time."
icon: "clipboard-check"
---

Begin only after the software gate passes for the exact reviewed revision.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose one robot and owner">
    Record model, serial identity, firmware, controller, SDK, network,
    workcell, operator, safety owner, and rollback procedure.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run read-only">
    Compare UMP identity, timestamps, units, frames, health, battery, safety,
    activity, and freshness against the manufacturer's native tooling.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test communication failure">
    Disconnect and restart UMP while confirming the robot remains safe and
    locally controllable under its native policy.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select one capability">
    Choose one bounded high-level native API. Qualify it in the vendor's
    supported simulator and retain fixtures and evidence.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Grant short authority">
    Issue one exact capability lease to one reviewed coordinator for one
    supervised window.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Execute in a controlled area">
    Use a bounded workspace, trained operator, physical emergency stop, and
    independent observation. Abort on any semantic mismatch.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review before expansion">
    Reconcile every outcome and fault. Add robots or capabilities one at a
    time, repeating the full gate after changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  A successful first trial qualifies only the reviewed robot, adapter,
  firmware, configuration, capability, and environment. It does not generalize
  to other robots or deployments.
</Warning>
