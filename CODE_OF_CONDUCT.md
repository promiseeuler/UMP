# Code of Conduct

UMP contributors must participate respectfully, discuss ideas in good faith,
and focus criticism on technical work rather than people. Harassment,
discrimination, threats, disclosure of private information, and unsafe conduct
are not acceptable.

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to
protect safety, privacy, or a constructive engineering environment. Report
conduct concerns privately to the repository owners. Do not include robot-owner
credentials, telemetry, security reports, or other confidential deployment data.
